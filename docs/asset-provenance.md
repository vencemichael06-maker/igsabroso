## Curation and processing

The 13 retained project folders were inventoried by exact Drive folder and file
ID. Contact sheets were built from Drive thumbnails, then six visually distinct,
sharp images were selected per project with a primary exterior/render first when
available and useful interior/detail coverage afterward. Duplicates, blurred
files, screenshots with application chrome, turnover/group photographs, and
ceremony near-duplicates were excluded.

The 78 selected originals are stored only below ignored `tmp/` paths. Sharp
creates deterministic 640 px thumbnails at WebP quality 78 and 1600 px lightbox
images at WebP quality 84. Zemke's verified HEIC originals are first decoded by
the bundled `heif-convert` into ignored lossless PNG intermediates; Sharp then
performs the same resize and WebP output path. Neither originals nor
intermediates are published.

Per the approved scope amendment, Issa, Villacorte, and Baet are omitted because
their verified folders could not supply six usable images. No image is reused
across projects and no substitute source was introduced.
